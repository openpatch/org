**Open Projects for Assessment and Training of Computing Competencies**

We build open source tools that improve the assessment and training of
competencies in computer science education and subjects like mathematics. We
hope that your and our work pushes everybody to reach their potential.

## This is Us

OpenPatch is an acronym for **Open** **P**rojects for **A**ssessment and
**T**raining of **C**ompetencies, especially in the field of computer science.
The four letters in **Open** are not only the first you read, but are also the
core foundation which we are based on. We believe that education should be
available to everybody. Therefore, we focus our work on open source, making
everything we do available to the public.

## Our Principles

Four beliefs guide every project we build. They are described in detail on the
[principles page](/principles).

- **[Put the student first](/principles#put-the-student-first)** — everything we
  do is for the student and must improve their learning.
- **[Develop in the open](/principles#develop-in-the-open)** — materials are
  never perfect, but keeping them to yourself is not a solution.
- **[Be a guide, not an inspector](/principles#be-a-guide-not-an-inspector)** —
  students should not be hindered by arbitrary borders.
- **[Digital tools can never replace a teacher](/principles#digital-tools-can-never-replace-a-teacher)**
  — our tools help teachers do their job, they do not replace them.
