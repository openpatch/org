# Learningmap

-![Learningmap logo](/images/logos/learningmap-plain-mixed.svg){width=120}

Learningmap is an open source tool for creating, sharing and exploring learning
paths. A map shows students where they are, what they have already mastered and
which steps they can take next.

## Highlights

- Learning paths — model a topic as a map of connected learning steps.
- Share and explore — publish a map and let students work through it at their
  own pace.
- Embeddable — drop a map straight into a Hyperbook page.
